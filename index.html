3. Custom Assembly Rules (1 – 7)

Rule 1 (Width > Standard, No Edging)

Determine required rolls/pieces. When custom width exceeds standard width, apply seam adhesive along the joining length.

Rule 2 (Width > Standard, All 4 Sides Edging – Full Perimeter)

Compute seam adhesive per Rule 1. Calculate perimeter edging length = (2 × Width) + (2 × Length). Apply chosen reducer profile and edging adhesive along all 4 outer edges.

Rule 3 (Width > Standard, 2 Width Sides – Traffic Entrance & Exit Ends)

Compute seam adhesive per Rule 1. Calculate edging length = 2 × Width. Apply reducer along the 2 width traffic ends only. (Corridor variant: 2 Length Sides = 2 × Length along walkway borders).

Rule 4 (Oversized Width & Length, No Edging)

Compute full roll segments and partial cuts to cover total oversized area. Calculate seam adhesive along length AND width seams. No perimeter edging applied.

Rule 5 (Oversized Width & Length, All 4 Sides Edging – Full Perimeter)

Follow Rule 4 for seam adhesive along length and width seams. Calculate perimeter edging length = (2 × Width) + (2 × Length). Apply reducer profile and edging adhesive along all 4 outer edges.

Rule 6 (Oversized Width & Length, 2 Width Sides – Traffic Entrance & Exit Ends)

Follow Rule 4 for seam adhesive along length and width seams. Calculate edging length = 2 × Width. Apply reducer along 2 width traffic ends. (Corridor variant: 2 Length Sides = 2 × Length along walkway borders).

Rule 7 (Wet Area Mat Special Exception – 3 ft × 40 ft):
  • Maximum width is limited strictly to 3 feet.
  • Standard chargeable lengths allowed: strictly 2 ft, 4 ft, 8 ft, and 10 ft.
  • Round-Up Rule: Any custom length input between standard sizes automatically rounds UP to the next standard allowable length for cost calculations.
  • Leftover cuts are non-reusable; charged area = 3 ft × rounded allowable length.
  • No seam adhesive or perimeter edging applied.
